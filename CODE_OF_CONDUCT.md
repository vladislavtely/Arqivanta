# Code of conduct

Arqivanta welcomes contributors of all backgrounds, identities, nationalities, abilities, and experience levels. Treat people with respect, critique work constructively, and make room for questions and different perspectives.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, and disclosure of someone’s private information without consent are unacceptable. These standards apply in project discussions, contributions, events, and places where someone represents the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer. Share only the details needed for review. Maintainers should protect reporter confidentiality where possible, respond without retaliation, and act proportionately. They may remove material, warn participants, restrict access, or ban repeat or severe offenders.
